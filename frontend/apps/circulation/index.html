Circulation

The heart, arteries and veins in 3D, and the routes hormones take through them. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click any vessel to learn about it.

Show

Hormones have no vessels of their own: glands release them straight into the blood, and the circulation carries them everywhere. Two routes are special portal systems, where blood passes through a second organ before returning to the heart: the hepatic portal vein (pancreas and gut → liver) and the tiny hypothalamus → pituitary portal vessels.
